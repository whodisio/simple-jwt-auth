## .caveat — one root behavior change

`createSecureDistributedAuthToken` now throws `SimpleJwtAuthError` when the private key's type does not fit the alg (an ec key under `RS256`, an rsa key under `ES256`). before, it signed, and the token verified against the wrong-type key. a caller that relied on that mismatch breaks; the pr body flags it for the commander (fulcrum F7).
